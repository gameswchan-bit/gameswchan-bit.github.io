QuickBite — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

What we collect

QuickBite uses your Google account to sign you in. When you sign in, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo (optional). We also store your food orders (items, quantities, and order history) so you can view and re-order.

How we use it

  • To create and manage your fast food orders
  • To identify your account when you sign in
  • To send order confirmations (if you enable notifications)

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your Google Drive, Calendar, or email content. We do not show ads based on your Google profile.

Data sharing

Your order details are shared with the restaurant or chain preparing your order. No other third parties receive your personal data.

Data retention

Your account and order history are kept while your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us.
